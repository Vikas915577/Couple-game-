# Changelog — YaarJam V1.2

## Added
- Dedicated Adults 18+ section with an age-confirmation gate.
- Darker premium after-hours visual theme, separate from the all-ages/friends shelf.
- Adult Truth or Dare (Friends) and Couples After Dark cards connect to existing playable Truth or Dare room modes.
- Five Coming Soon adult-game previews: Spin the Bottle 18+, After-Dark Would You Rather, Couples Challenge Roulette, Adult Quiz Battle, and Mystery Dare Wheel. These cards are non-interactive until their game logic is ready.
- Adult-zone unit tests validate unique IDs, allowed statuses, and that only existing playable catalogue games are launchable.

## Preserved
- Tic Tac Toe online and local computer opponents (Easy / Medium / Hard).
- Room chat component, Ludo panel/logic, party games, Supabase integration, and Truth or Dare prompt packs.
- Existing room codes, current room flows, and responsive layout.

## Guardrails
- Adults zone requires an explicit 18+ confirmation. Adult prompt packs also require a separate in-game confirmation.
- Consent-first copy makes skip/pass available and never encourages pressure, private-photo sharing, or sharing sensitive personal details.
- Coming Soon cards are explicitly disabled so they cannot create empty rooms.
