# YaarJam V1.1 changelog

## Added
- Tic Tac Toe vs Computer: Easy / Medium / Hard difficulty, with minimax for Hard.
- Local Ludo vs Computer / same-phone mode and online 2–4 player Ludo with server-side dice and move validation after migration.
- Room text chat with recent-message history, realtime updates, membership checks, a 500-character limit and send-rate guard.
- Truth or Dare packs: Classic, Funny, Romantic, Flirty, Spicy 18+ and After Dark 18+, with extra prompts and visible adult confirmation.
- Local playable party games: Would You Rather, This or That, Never Have I Ever, Most Likely To, Guess the Word, Partner Quiz, Our Story Quiz and Rock Paper Scissors.
- Additional unit tests for the AI, Ludo rules, online Ludo state normalization and prompt packs.

## Kept honest / intentionally not included yet
- No voice calling, no online party-game voting sync, and no claim of online Ludo until the SQL migration and multi-device tests pass.
- Existing V1 games, data model and selected Supabase project are preserved.
